Why Sponsor Us?

See how your company can benefit from supporting Artrobots.

Who We Are

We are Artrobots — a team passionate about technology, innovation, and learning. Our goal is to build robotics projects that challenge creativity and engineering limits, compete in events, and promote hands-on learning among our members.

What We Do

  • Robotics Projects: We build robots for competitions and technical challenges.
  • Education & Training: We provide workshops and training for our members and the community.
  • Competing: We represent our institution in major robotics events such as RSM and RoboChallenge.

Visibility We Offer

By sponsoring Artrobots, your brand gets:

  • Robot exposure: Your logo displayed prominently on robots used in competitions.
  • Team shirts: Your logo on official Artrobots shirts.
  • Social media: Promotion on our social channels, including Instagram (artrobots.inteli) and LinkedIn.
  • Events: Brand presence on banners and promotional materials at events we attend.
  • Marketing materials: Your logo included in flyers, presentations, and other promotional materials.
